College Project • Arduino Embedded System Hardware Prototype

Anti-Sleep Alarm System for Drivers

Active Real-Time Drowsiness Detection via Eye-Blink Sensor Interfacing

The Problem

Driver drowsiness, exhaustion, and sudden micro-sleep episodes during long night commutes cause catastrophic road accidents. Most commercial vehicles lack automated fatigue prevention.

Engineered Solution

Engineered an embedded prototype that detects driver drowsiness by monitoring continuous eye-blinking via an optical eye-blink sensor. Programmed Arduino microcontroller logic to trigger a high-frequency buzzer alarm when prolonged eye closure is detected, warning the driver without seizing vehicle control.

My Hands-on Contributions & Features:

  • Circuit Assembly: Wired Arduino board, optical eye-blink sensor, piezo buzzer, 9V battery, and breadboard.
  • Embedded Algorithm: Programmed logic to differentiate between rapid natural blinks and continuous fatigue closure.
  • Safety-Critical Design: System warns the driver instantly with audible alerts and purposefully leaves vehicular control in the driver's hands.
  • Practical Exposure: Gained hands-on knowledge in microcontroller logic, sensor interfacing, and automated alert systems.

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Idea • AgTech Platform Architecture Concept

FarmConnect — Farmer-to-Consumer Platform

Decentralized Hub Distribution & Fair-Trade Direct Agricultural Marketplace

The Problem

Agricultural supply chains are congested with layered middlemen and commission brokers who consume upwards of 40-60% of produce value, while consumers receive delayed, stale produce at elevated retail prices.

Proposed Platform Architecture

Proposed a direct platform concept connecting consumers directly with farmers, removing extra layers between producer and buyer. Designed a localized hub distribution model to streamline aggregation, quality inspection, and prompt last-mile dispatch.

Key Contributions & Architecture:

  • SIH Problem Identification: Formulated economic analysis around farmer earnings, freshness loss, and logistics barriers.
  • Produce Catalog Planning: Structured a diverse multi-category catalogue for vegetables, fresh milk, curd, eggs, rice, pulses, and organic crops.
  • Hub-and-Spoke Logistics: Designed village-level collection points delivering to local urban hubs, drastically shrinking turnaround time.
